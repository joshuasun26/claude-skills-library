# Run your email from your phone, by voice

The heavy work runs on your computer. Your phone is just the remote and the microphone.

## Setup (once)
1. Keep your computer on, with Claude Code (or the Claude desktop app) open and signed in.
2. Turn on remote access for the session, so the Claude app on your phone can see and drive it. In Claude Code this is **Remote Control**; check the current Claude Code docs for how to enable it in your version.
3. Install the Claude app on your phone and sign in to the same account.

## Daily use
1. On your computer (or from your phone), start a Reading Desk session after a triage run: "start a reading desk."
2. On your phone, open that session.
3. Use your phone keyboard's microphone (or the app's voice input) and talk:
   - "Next."
   - "Reply: tell her Thursday works, and thank her for the photos."
   - "Shorter." / "Warmer." / "Leave out the price."
   - "Skip this one, I'll handle it on my computer."
   - "Save that draft."
4. Drafts save to your email drafts folder. Send them yourself from your phone's mail app or your computer when you have read them.

## Tips
- Talk the way you would to an assistant. The voice profile turns your spoken notes into your written voice.
- Dictation mishears names and numbers. Read every draft before you send, especially amounts, dates and addresses.
- If your phone loses the session, nothing is lost: the desk list lives in a file, so just reopen it and say "where were we?"

## Honest limits
- Your computer has to be on and awake. If it sleeps, the phone cannot reach the session.
- Remote features and voice input change between app versions. If a step above does not match your screen, follow the current Claude docs for remote access.
