# Ways to read your email with AI

Different days need different modes. All of them work from the same triage list and all of them only draft.

1. **Reading Desk (one at a time).** The calmest mode. A numbered list, newest first. One email at a time with what it needs and a draft reply. Say "next." Best for clearing a backlog or a morning pass.
2. **Digest.** A short summary after each triage run, saved to a file and shown in chat (or texted to you, if you have a texting tool connected): how many need an answer, who you are waiting on, anything urgent. Best for staying aware without opening anything. Inbox Triage's `notify` setting controls this.
3. **One bucket only.** Ask for a single list: "show me just who I'm waiting on" or "only the ones that need an answer today." Best when you have ten minutes.
4. **Batch drafts.** "Draft replies to everything in needs-an-answer, then show me the list." You skim and approve. Fastest, but read every draft before sending.
5. **By voice from your phone.** The Reading Desk, driven by dictation. See `PHONE-DICTATION.md` in this folder.

Pick one as your default and use the others when the day calls for it.
